# Generator Coil Guide V5.1 Design

## Scope

Revise the stationary generator package and lower magnet rotor without changing
the accepted rotor blade or bayonet geometry.

## Coil cassette

- Replace the empty winding annulus with 18 rounded, open-top serpentine guide
  islands following the functional layout measured in the supplied reference
  `2 Coil_Former.stl`.
- Keep the guide open for experimental wire diameters and turn counts, including
  the user's existing 0.18 mm enamelled copper wire.
- Keep the stationary cassette clear of the rotating central sleeve and retain
  the existing keyed, radially piloted, cover-retained cassette interface.
- Route both winding leads to the existing non-waterproof side cable passage.
- Treat the represented winding as an envelope, not a validated electrical
  design or a model of individual wires.

## Housing and cover fasteners

- Use exactly four M4 cover fasteners at 0, 90, 180 and 270 degrees.
- Put each M4 boss on the same radial axis as one separate lower frame-mount
  hole; the cover fastener must not also fasten the housing to the wood frame.
- Connect each boss structurally to the wall and bottom mounting tab so the boss
  reinforces the tab.
- Preserve top driver access, captive nut access from below, and independent
  access to the frame screw holes.

## Lower magnet rotor sleeve

- Fuse the existing 12 mm OD rotating spacer envelope to the top of the lower
  magnet rotor.
- Preserve the 8.8 mm M8 clearance passage and rear-open captive M8 nut pocket.
- Extend the fused sleeve from the lower magnet face to the lower face of the
  upper M8 nut, currently 17.85 mm nominal height.
- Remove the separate printable spacer from exports, assembly inventory and
  documentation while retaining explicit collision auditing of the fused sleeve.

## Constraints

- Nominal upper and lower magnet-face-to-active-winding gaps remain 1.5 mm.
- Housing, cassette, cover and winding stay stationary; both magnet rotors,
  shaft, nuts and fused sleeve rotate.
- The result remains a non-waterproof, physically uncalibrated prototype.
- Generated STL, STEP, drawings, README and all released manuals must agree with
  the revised CAD.
