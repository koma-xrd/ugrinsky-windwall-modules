# Plain blade ends

Adjacent rotor modules lock at a 60-degree blade phase. Their blade walls end
at the nominal module planes, so the nominal axial gap at each blade-end
interface is zero.

The central three-lug bayonet is the only inter-module locking and torque
transfer path. The plain blade ends provide aerodynamic continuity only; they
do not provide keyed load sharing.

Inspect every printed joint physically before use. Confirm the blade-wall ends
meet flush at the nominal phase, the bayonet reaches its permanent stops, and
the print material, fit, wear, balance, load capacity and operating conditions
are suitable for the intended installation. CAD checks and coupon exports do
not certify those properties.
